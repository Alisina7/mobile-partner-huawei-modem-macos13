Installation complete

Restart your Mac now.

  1. After restarting, open Mobile Partner from Applications.
  2. Enter the APN supplied by your mobile carrier (for example internet).
  3. Press Connect.

While connected, the E1550 provides the Mac’s internet connection. Disconnect from the app when you are done.

If the app still says Driver inactive after the restart, use Diagnostics in the app and check:

/var/log/mobilepartner.log — helper log
/var/log/mobilepartner-install.log — installer log

The original Mobile Partner app is not used and can be discarded.
